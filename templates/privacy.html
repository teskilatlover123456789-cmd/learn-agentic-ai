Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, such as your name, email address, and the financial transaction logs you enter into the app. We also collect basic technical information such as your browser type and IP address for security and performance optimization.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide the expense tracking service. We use your transaction logs to generate your spending reports and summaries. Your email is used for account management and authentication.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard encryption and security protocols to prevent unauthorized access. We only retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data to outside parties. We may use trusted third-party infrastructure providers (such as hosting services) who are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request the deletion of your data, please contact us at privacy@spendly.example.com.
